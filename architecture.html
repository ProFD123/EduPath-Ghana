DESIGN & ENGINEERING

Architecture

Architecture combines design, technology and building science to create functional and sustainable spaces.

About the Programme

Architecture involves the planning and design of buildings and other physical environments.

Students develop creative, technical and analytical skills while learning about design, construction, materials and the built environment.

Main Areas of Study

Architectural Design Building Technology Architectural Drawing Construction History of Architecture Environmental Design Computer-Aided Design Urban Design

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Courses

Visual Arts, General Science, Technical & Vocational

🎨 Key Skills

Creativity, drawing, mathematics, design and problem-solving.

⏱️ Duration

Duration varies according to the institution and professional pathway.

🏗️ Work Environment

Architecture firms, construction companies, planning organisations and government agencies.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
