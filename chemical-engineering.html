ENGINEERING

Chemical Engineering

Chemical Engineering applies chemistry, mathematics, physics and engineering principles to design and improve processes used to produce useful products on a large scale.

About the Programme

Chemical Engineering focuses on the design, development and operation of industrial processes that transform raw materials into useful products.

Students learn how scientific and engineering principles can be used in areas such as energy, food processing, pharmaceuticals, petrochemicals, materials, water treatment and manufacturing.

Areas You May Study

Chemical Engineering Principles Organic Chemistry Physical Chemistry Process Engineering Thermodynamics Fluid Mechanics Heat Transfer Mass Transfer Process Control Reaction Engineering Materials Science Plant Design

Entry Requirements

WASSCE

Applicants normally need the required WASSCE subjects and grades specified by the university offering the programme.

Important Subjects

Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry are particularly important for Chemical Engineering. English Language and other required subjects also apply according to the university's admission requirements.

Universities Offering Chemical Engineering

KNUST — BSc Chemical Engineering
Kumasi Technical University (KsTU) — Bachelor of Engineering in Chemical Engineering

Skills You Can Develop

Problem Solving

Develop the ability to analyse complex engineering problems and develop practical solutions.

Mathematical Skills

Use mathematics and quantitative methods to understand and solve engineering problems.

Process Design

Learn how industrial processes and systems can be designed, evaluated and improved.

Research & Analysis

Develop skills in experimentation, data analysis and technical research.

Who Is This Programme For?

Chemical Engineering may be suitable for students who enjoy Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics and are interested in technology, manufacturing, problem solving and how products are produced on a large scale.
