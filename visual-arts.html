SHS COURSE

Visual Arts

Develop your creativity, artistic skills and design knowledge while exploring opportunities in art, design, fashion and related fields.

What is Visual Arts?

Visual Arts is an SHS pathway that develops creative, artistic and practical skills. Students explore different forms of art and design while learning how to communicate ideas visually and creatively.

Common Subjects

English Language Core Mathematics Integrated Science Social Studies Graphic Design General Knowledge in Art Picture Making Textiles Sculpture Phography ICT Art History and Appreciation Ceramic and Pottery Interio Design Physical Education & Health

Who is Visual Arts suitable for?

🎨 Creative Students

Students who enjoy drawing, creating, designing and expressing ideas visually.

💡 Idea Builders

Students who enjoy turning ideas and concepts into creative projects.

🖥️ Design Enthusiasts

Students interested in graphic design, digital creativity and visual communication.

✂️ Practical Learners

Students who enjoy learning through practical activities and creative work.
