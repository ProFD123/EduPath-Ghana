SUBJECTS

Prepare for your subjects

Focus your revision on the subjects that matter most to your programme and goals.

🔢 Mathematics

Practise calculations, formulas, graphs, algebra, geometry and problem-solving.

🔬 Integrated Science

Review important scientific concepts and practise applying them to examination questions.

📘 English Language

Improve comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, summary and writing skills.

🌍 Social Studies

Review important social, economic, political and environmental topics.

⚗️ Chemistry

Revise chemical concepts, equations, calculations, experiments and applications.

🧬 Biology

Study living systems, human biology, ecology, genetics and other important concepts.

⚡ Physics

Practise mechanics, electricity, waves, energy and calculations.

💼 Business

Review business concepts, accounting, economics and entrepreneurship.

PAST QUESTIONS

Practise before the examination

Past questions can help you understand question patterns and identify areas that need more revision.
📝

Practice is important

Try answering questions under timed conditions. After marking your work, review every question you got wrong and learn why your answer was incorrect.

STUDY TIPS

Study more effectively

01

Create a study timetable

Divide your available study time between your different subjects.

02

Practise regularly

Don't wait until examinations are close before you begin practising questions.

03

Review your mistakes

Mistakes can show you exactly which topics need more attention.

04

Take breaks

Short breaks can help you stay focused during longer study sessions.

05

Ask for help

Ask teachers, classmates or trusted adults when you don't understand a topic.

06

Stay consistent

Regular study over time is more useful than trying to learn everything at once.

REVISION GUIDE

Build your revision plan

📅 Plan

List your subjects and decide what topics you need to revise each week.

📖 Learn

Understand the topic before trying to memorise information.

📝 Practise

Answer practice and past questions regularly.

🔁 Review

Return to difficult topics and correct your previous mistakes.

EXAM PREPARATION

Prepare with confidence

Before the exam

  • Revise consistently.
  • Practise examination questions.
  • Know your examination timetable.
  • Prepare the materials you are allowed to use.

During the exam

  • Read the instructions carefully.
  • Manage your time.
  • Answer questions carefully.
  • Review your work when time permits.

After the exam

  • Move on to preparing for your next paper.
  • Learn from areas you found difficult.
  • Keep your long-term academic goals in mind.

EXAM STRATEGIES

Smart examination habits

Read the question carefully. Make sure you understand what the question is asking.
Manage your time. Avoid spending too much time on one difficult question.
Show your working where necessary. This is especially important for calculation-based questions.
Check your answers. Use any remaining time to review your work.
