HEALTH SCIENCE

Nursing

Nursing focuses on caring for patients, promoting health, preventing illness and supporting recovery.

About the Programme

Nursing is a healthcare profession that prepares students to provide safe, compassionate and evidence-based care to individuals, families and communities.

Students learn about human health, disease, patient care, health promotion, clinical practice and professional nursing responsibilities.

Main Areas of Study

Anatomy Physiology Pharmacology Medical-Surgical Nursing Community Health Maternal Health Child Health Mental Health Nursing

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Course

General Science

⏱️ Duration

Duration varies according to the nursing programme and institution.

❤️ Recommended Background

Students interested in Biology, healthcare, patient care and working with people.

🏥 Work Environment

Hospitals, clinics, community health facilities, schools, public-health organisations and other healthcare settings.

Typical WASSCE Requirements

Requirements vary between institutions. Science-based nursing programmes commonly require credits in English Language, Core Mathematics and Integrated Science together with relevant science electives.

Always check the current admission requirements of the institution you intend to attend.
