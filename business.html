SHS COURSE

Business

Develop knowledge in business, accounting, economics and entrepreneurship while preparing for further studies and careers in the business world.

What is Business?

Business is an SHS pathway that introduces students to areas such as accounting, economics, management, entrepreneurship and business operations. It can provide a foundation for many business-related university programmes and careers.

Common Subjects

English Language Core Mathematics Integrated Science Social Studies Financial Accounting Business Management Economics Cost Accounting Additional Mathematics Entrepreneurship ICT Physical Education & Health

Who is Business suitable for?

💰 Finance Lovers

Students interested in money, accounting, financial records and how organisations manage resources.

📊 Analytical Thinkers

Students who enjoy working with numbers, information and solving business problems.

🚀 Future Entrepreneurs

Students interested in starting, managing or developing businesses.

🏢 Future Business Professionals

Students considering careers in banking, management, accounting, marketing and finance.
