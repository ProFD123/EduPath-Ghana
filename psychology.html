HUMANITIES & SOCIAL SCIENCE

Psychology

Psychology studies human behaviour, mental processes and how people think, learn and interact.

About the Programme

Psychology explores human behaviour and mental processes using scientific research and evidence.

Students learn about development, learning, social behaviour, personality, research methods and psychological assessment.

Main Areas of Study

Developmental Psychology Social Psychology Cognitive Psychology Personality Research Methods Biological Psychology Educational Psychology Industrial Psychology

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Courses

General Arts, General Science

⏱️ Typical Duration

Usually around 4 years for a bachelor's degree.

🧠 Key Skills

Research, communication, observation and critical thinking.

🏢 Work Environment

Schools, businesses, research organisations, community programmes and social services.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
