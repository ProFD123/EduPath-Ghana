General Science

Explore the sciences, understand how the world works and prepare for a wide range of university programmes and careers.

What is General Science?

General Science is an SHS pathway that focuses on scientific knowledge, problem-solving and practical understanding. It is suitable for students who are interested in areas such as health, engineering, technology and the natural sciences.

Common Subjects

English Language Core Mathematics Physical Education & Health Social Studies Physics Chemistry Biology Additional Mathematics Agriculture ICT Computer Science Food & Nutrition

Who is General Science suitable for?

🧠 Science Lovers

Students who enjoy understanding how things work and solving scientific problems.

🔢 Mathematics Students

Students who are comfortable with mathematics and logical problem-solving.

🔬 Future Scientists

Students interested in biology, chemistry, physics or other scientific fields.

💡 Future Professionals

Students considering careers in health, engineering, technology and related areas.
