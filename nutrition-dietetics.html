HEALTH SCIENCE

Nutrition & Dietetics

Nutrition and Dietetics explores food, nutrients and their relationship with health and wellbeing.

About the Programme

Nutrition and Dietetics combines biological and health sciences with the study of food and nutrition.

Students learn how nutrition affects health and how appropriate dietary practices can support individuals, families and communities.

Main Areas of Study

Human Nutrition Food Science Clinical Nutrition Community Nutrition Biochemistry Food Safety Dietetics Nutrition Assessment
