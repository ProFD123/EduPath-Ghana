COMPUTING & TECHNOLOGY

Information Technology

Information Technology focuses on computer systems, networks, software and the technology used to manage digital information.

About the Programme

Information Technology prepares students to work with computer hardware, software, networks, databases and technology infrastructure.

Students develop practical and technical skills for implementing, maintaining and supporting information technology systems.

Main Areas of Study

Computer Networks Programming Database Systems Web Development Operating Systems Cloud Computing IT Support Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Courses

Computing & ICT, General Science, Business

⏱️ Typical Duration

Usually around 4 years for a bachelor's degree.

💻 Recommended Skills

Problem-solving, logical thinking, technology interest and willingness to learn new tools.

🏢 Work Environment

Technology companies, banks, schools, government organisations and businesses.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
