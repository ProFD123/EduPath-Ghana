SHS COURSE

General Arts

Explore society, history, languages, culture, government and human behaviour while preparing for a wide range of university programmes.

What is General Arts?

General Arts is an SHS pathway that focuses on subjects related to people, society, culture, communication, history and the humanities. It can prepare students for many programmes in the humanities, social sciences, law, education, communication and related fields.

Common Subjects

English Language Core Mathematics Integrated Science Social Studies Government Economics Geography History Literature in English French CRS Music ICT Twi Physical Education & Health Additional Mathematics

Who is General Arts suitable for?

🗣️ Communication Lovers

Students who enjoy reading, writing, communication and expressing ideas.

🌍 Social Studies Lovers

Students interested in people, society, geography, history and current affairs.

⚖️ Future Legal Professionals

Students considering law and other fields connected to justice and society.

🎓 Future Social Scientists

Students interested in understanding people, communities and institutions.
