HEALTH SCIENCE

Public Health

Public Health focuses on protecting and improving the health of communities and populations.

About the Programme

Public Health focuses on preventing disease, promoting healthy living and improving health outcomes within communities and populations.

Students learn how health problems can be investigated, prevented and managed through education, research, policy and community-based interventions.

Main Areas of Study

Epidemiology Biostatistics Health Promotion Environmental Health Community Health Public Health Policy Research Methods Disease Prevention
