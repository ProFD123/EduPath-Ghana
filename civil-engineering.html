About Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering is a field of engineering concerned with the planning, design, construction and maintenance of infrastructure. It involves applying mathematics, science and engineering principles to solve real-world problems and create safe and useful structures.

🏢 Structural Engineering

🛣️ Transportation

📐 Engineering Design

Develop skills in engineering design, calculations and problem solving.
