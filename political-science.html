SOCIAL SCIENCES

Political Science

Political Science is the study of government, politics, political institutions, public policy and the relationship between citizens and the state.

About the Programme

Political Science helps students understand how governments operate, how political decisions are made and how political institutions influence society.

Students develop analytical, research, writing and communication skills that can be useful in government, public administration, international organisations, research, journalism and other fields.

Areas You May Study

Political Theory Ghanaian Politics Comparative Politics International Relations Public Policy Public Administration Political Institutions Human Rights Research Methods Governance

Entry Requirements

WASSCE

Applicants normally need the required WASSCE subjects and grades specified by the university offering the programme.

Important Subjects

English Language and Social Studies are useful foundations. Requirements for elective subjects and grades vary between universities.

Skills You Can Develop

Critical Thinking

Learn to analyse political ideas, events and public issues from different perspectives.

Research

Develop skills for collecting, analysing and presenting information.

Communication

Improve written and spoken communication for academic and professional settings.

Policy Analysis

Learn how policies can be examined and evaluated.

Who Is This Programme For?

Political Science may be suitable for students who are interested in government, leadership, society, current affairs, international relations, research and understanding how decisions affect people.
