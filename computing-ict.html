Computing & ICT

Develop digital skills and understand computing, information technology, programming and modern technology.

What is Computing & ICT?

Computing and ICT introduces students to computers, digital technologies, information systems and problem-solving. Students can develop skills that are useful for further studies and careers in technology.

Areas You May Explore

Computer Programming Information Technology Computer Systems Databases Web Development Networking Digital Technology Data & Information

Who is Computing & ICT suitable for?

💻 Technology Lovers

Students who enjoy computers, technology and discovering how digital systems work.

🧠 Problem Solvers

Students who enjoy logical thinking, solving problems and creating solutions.

🌐 Digital Creators

Students interested in websites, applications, digital tools and technology.

🚀 Future Tech Professionals

Students considering careers in software, computing, data and information technology.
