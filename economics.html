Economics

Economics studies how individuals, businesses and governments make decisions about limited resources.

About the Programme

Economics examines production, consumption, markets, employment, inflation, development and economic policy.

Students use economic theory, mathematics, statistics and research to understand real-world economic problems.

Main Areas of Study

Microeconomics Macroeconomics Statistics Econometrics Development Economics International Economics Public Finance Economic Policy
