Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Welcome to EduPath Ghana. We respect the privacy of our visitors and students. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, how it may be used and how it may be protected.

1. Information We Collect

When students create accounts or use our services, we may collect information such as:

  • Names entered during account registration.
  • Login details needed to access student accounts.
  • Information students voluntarily submit through website features, such as reviews or feedback.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, where this information is made available to us.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically required and the website explains why it is needed.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Allow students to log in and use available services.
  • Display or manage student reviews and feedback.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Respond to questions, reports or requests.
  • Protect the website against misuse and unauthorized access.

3. Student Accounts and Passwords

Students are responsible for keeping their login details private and should not share their passwords with other people. EduPath Ghana should handle passwords using appropriate security protections and should never display passwords publicly.

If you believe someone has accessed your account without permission, contact the website team through the contact method provided on the site.

4. Reviews and User Submissions

If you submit a review, comment or other content through EduPath Ghana, the information you provide may be processed to operate that feature. Do not include private information in public submissions. Where a submission is displayed publicly, it may be visible to other visitors.

5. Cookies and Technical Information

The website or services used to operate it may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functions, remember preferences or understand website usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect certain features.

6. Third-Party Services and External Links

EduPath Ghana may use third-party hosting, technical services or links to external educational websites. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage visitors to read the privacy policies of external websites before using them.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell students' personal information. Information may be accessed by people responsible for operating and maintaining the website, or disclosed where necessary to protect the website, comply with applicable law or respond to a valid legal request.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information should only be kept for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

9. Children's and Students' Privacy

EduPath Ghana is an educational platform intended to support learners. Students should provide only information necessary to use the website. Younger students should seek help from a parent, guardian or trusted adult when creating accounts or sharing information online.

10. Your Choices

You should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through public areas of the website. If you want to ask about information associated with your account or request a correction, contact the EduPath Ghana team using the contact details provided on the website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact the EduPath Ghana team through the contact method provided on our website.
