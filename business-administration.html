About Business Administration

Business Administration focuses on how organisations are managed and how businesses operate successfully. Students develop knowledge in areas such as management, finance, marketing, accounting and entrepreneurship.

What You May Learn

📊 Management

Learn how organisations are managed, including planning, leadership and decision-making.

💰 Finance

Understand financial planning, budgeting and the management of business resources.

📢 Marketing

Explore how businesses promote products, understand customers and build brands.

🚀 Entrepreneurship

Develop knowledge about creating, managing and growing businesses.
