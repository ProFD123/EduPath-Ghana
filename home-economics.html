SHS COURSE

Home Economics

Develop practical knowledge and skills in food, nutrition, clothing, hospitality and other areas of everyday life.

What is Home Economics?

Home Economics is an SHS pathway that combines practical skills with knowledge in areas such as food and nutrition, clothing and textiles, and management of resources. It can prepare students for further studies and careers in hospitality, fashion, nutrition, education and related fields.

Common Subjects

English Language Core Mathematics Integrated Science Social Studies Food & Nutrition Management in Living Clothing & Textiles Economics

Who is Home Economics suitable for?

🍳 Food & Nutrition Lovers

Students interested in food, nutrition, healthy living and food preparation.

👗 Fashion Enthusiasts

Students who enjoy clothing, textiles, fashion and creative practical work.

🏨 Hospitality Enthusiasts

Students interested in hospitality, catering, tourism and related services.

🛠️ Practical Learners

Students who enjoy learning through practical activities and projects.
