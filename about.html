Who We Are

EduPath Ghana is an educational platform created to support students in Ghana by making useful academic information and learning resources easier to explore.

We believe that access to the right information can help students make informed decisions about their education and future careers. Our goal is to provide a helpful online space where learners can discover resources, explore opportunities and develop their potential.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make educational information more accessible to Ghanaian students and support them as they progress through their academic journey.

What We Offer

JHS Resources

Access learning resources and academic information designed to support Junior High School students.

SHS Resources

Explore study materials, curriculum resources and information to support Senior High School learning.

Scholarships

Discover scholarship information and educational opportunities that may help students pursue their ambitions.

Career Guidance

Explore career information and learn about possible educational and professional pathways.

Review Arena

Participate in student reviews and share useful feedback through available website features.

Student Accounts

Create an account and access the student features available on the EduPath Ghana platform.

Our Vision

We envision an environment where every Ghanaian student can confidently explore educational opportunities, access helpful learning resources and make informed decisions about the future.

Our Commitment

We are committed to improving the platform, making information easier to access and creating a useful experience for students. We encourage learners to verify important academic and admission information with the relevant official institutions.
