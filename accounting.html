BUSINESS & FINANCE

Accounting

Accounting focuses on recording, analysing and reporting financial information for organisations and individuals.

About the Programme

Accounting teaches students how financial information is prepared, analysed and used to support business decisions.

Main Areas of Study

Financial Accounting Management Accounting Auditing Taxation Financial Management Business Law Economics Accounting Information Systems

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Courses

Business, General Arts, General Science

⏱️ Typical Duration

Usually around 4 years for a bachelor's degree.

📊 Key Skills

Numeracy, analysis, organisation and attention to detail.

🏢 Work Environment

Banks, accounting firms, companies, government agencies and NGOs.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
