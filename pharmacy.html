HEALTH SCIENCE

Pharmacy

Pharmacy focuses on medicines, their development, preparation, safe use and their effects on the human body.

About the Programme

Pharmacy is a health-science field that combines chemistry, biology and pharmaceutical sciences to understand medicines and their proper use.

Students learn about medicines, how they are prepared and formulated, how they work in the body, and how they can be used safely and effectively to support patient care.

Main Areas of Study

Pharmaceutical Chemistry Pharmacology Pharmaceutics Pharmacognosy Pharmacy Practice Clinical Pharmacy Medicinal Chemistry Drug Safety

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Course

General Science

⏱️ Typical Duration

The Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) programme is typically a 6-year professional programme.

🧪 Recommended Background

Students interested in Chemistry, Biology, healthcare, medicines and scientific problem-solving may find Pharmacy suitable.

🏥 Work Environment

Pharmacies, hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, public-health organisations and other healthcare settings.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
