COMPUTING & TECHNOLOGY

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, applications and information from digital threats.

About the Programme

Cybersecurity prepares students to understand digital security risks and develop methods for protecting information systems.

Students study security principles, networks, risk management, digital forensics and defensive security practices.

Main Areas of Study

Network Security Information Security Digital Forensics Cryptography Risk Management Secure Systems Cyber Law Security Operations

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Courses

Computing & ICT, General Science

⏱️ Typical Duration

Usually around 4 years for a bachelor's degree.

🧠 Recommended Skills

Logical reasoning, problem-solving, computing interest and attention to detail.

🏢 Work Environment

Banks, technology companies, government agencies, telecommunications companies and security teams.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
