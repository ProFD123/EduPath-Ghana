ENGINEERING

Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering focuses on the design, development, manufacturing and maintenance of machines, mechanical systems and equipment.

What you will study

Mechanical Engineering combines mathematics, physics, materials science and engineering principles to help students understand how machines and mechanical systems work.

Engineering Mathematics Engineering Mechanics Thermodynamics Fluid Mechanics Machine Design Materials Science Manufacturing Processes Computer-Aided Design Heat Transfer Control Systems

Typical entry requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For WASSCE applicants, Mechanical Engineering generally requires strong results in the core subjects together with relevant science electives. Mathematics and Physics are particularly important.

Core subjects

English Language, Core Mathematics and Integrated Science are typically required.

Relevant electives

Physics and Elective Mathematics are commonly important, with Chemistry or other approved technical subjects depending on the university.

Always check the latest admission requirements of the university you want to apply to before making your application.

Universities that offer Mechanical Engineering

KNUST

Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology offers BSc Mechanical Engineering through its College of Engineering.

UMaT

University of Mines and Technology offers BSc Mechanical Engineering under its Faculty of Engineering.

KNUST Obuasi Campus

KNUST's Obuasi Campus also lists BSc Mechanical Engineering among its undergraduate programmes.

Where you can work

Manufacturing

Factories and manufacturing companies need engineers to design, operate and improve machines and production systems.

Automotive Industry

Mechanical engineers can work with vehicles, automobile systems, maintenance and manufacturing.

Energy

Engineers can work with power generation, renewable energy, mechanical equipment and energy systems.

Construction & Engineering

Mechanical engineers can contribute to large engineering projects and the installation and maintenance of equipment.
