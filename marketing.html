BUSINESS

Marketing

Marketing focuses on understanding customers, promoting products and services, building brands and developing strategies that help organisations grow.

What you will study

Marketing combines business knowledge, communication, creativity and research. Students learn how organisations understand their customers and develop strategies to reach them effectively.

Principles of Marketing Consumer Behaviour Marketing Research Advertising Digital Marketing Brand Management Sales Management Business Communication Business Statistics Strategic Marketing

Typical entry requirements

Requirements differ between universities and programmes. WASSCE applicants normally need the required passes in the core subjects together with the electives accepted by the university.

Core subjects

English Language, Core Mathematics and other required core subjects are normally considered for admission.

Useful electives

Depending on the university, relevant subjects may include Economics, Business Management, Accounting, Geography, Government or other approved electives.

Check the current admission requirements of your preferred university before applying.

Universities that offer Marketing

KNUST

Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology offers BSc Business Administration with Marketing.

University of Cape Coast

The University of Cape Coast offers Bachelor of Commerce (Marketing) and related Marketing programmes.

Other universities

Marketing and business-related programmes are also available at other Ghanaian universities. Programme names and admission requirements may differ.

Where you can work

Companies

Marketing graduates can work in private companies across industries such as technology, banking, manufacturing, retail and telecommunications.

Advertising & Media

Graduates can work in advertising agencies, media companies, public relations and marketing communications.

Digital Businesses

Digital marketing skills can lead to roles involving social media, online advertising, content and e-commerce.

Entrepreneurship

Marketing knowledge can also be useful for starting and growing a business by understanding customers and markets.
