Technical & Vocational

Build practical skills, technical knowledge and hands-on experience for further education, employment and entrepreneurship.

What is Technical & Vocational?

Technical and Vocational education focuses on practical skills and technical knowledge that can be applied to real-world situations. Students can develop skills in areas such as engineering, construction, electrical work, automotive technology, fashion and other practical fields.

Areas You May Explore

Electrical Installation Electronics Automotive Technology Building Construction Welding & Fabrication Mechanical Engineering Plumbing Carpentry & Joinery Fashion & Design

Who is this pathway suitable for?

🔧 Practical Learners

Students who enjoy building, repairing, creating and working with tools.

⚙️ Technical Thinkers

Students interested in understanding how machines, systems and technology work.

🏗️ Builders & Creators

Students who enjoy practical projects, construction and creating useful products.

🚀 Future Entrepreneurs

Students who want to develop practical skills that can support their own businesses.
