HUMANITIES & COMMUNICATION

Communication Studies

Communication Studies focuses on how people and organisations create, share and interpret information through different forms of communication.

About the Programme

Communication Studies prepares students to understand and use communication effectively in areas such as journalism, public relations, broadcasting, digital media, advertising and corporate communication.

Students develop skills in writing, research, public speaking, media production, communication strategy and critical thinking.

Areas You May Study

Communication Theory Journalism Public Relations Broadcasting Digital Media Advertising Media Studies Communication Research Public Speaking Writing

Entry Requirements

WASSCE

Applicants normally need the required WASSCE subjects and grades specified by the university offering the programme.

Important Subjects

English Language is particularly important. Depending on the university, applicants may also need relevant Social Studies, Mathematics and elective subjects.

Skills You Can Develop

Communication

Learn how to communicate ideas clearly to different audiences.

Writing & Research

Develop professional writing, research and information analysis skills.

Media Skills

Gain knowledge of traditional and digital media platforms.

Critical Thinking

Learn to analyse information, messages and communication strategies.

Who Is This Programme For?

Communication Studies may be a good choice for students who enjoy writing, speaking, media, creativity, research, working with people and sharing ideas.
