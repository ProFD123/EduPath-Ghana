HEALTH & SCIENCE

Medical Laboratory Science

Medical Laboratory Science combines biology, chemistry and laboratory science to help diagnose, monitor and understand diseases through scientific testing.

About the Programme

Medical Laboratory Science is a health-science programme that focuses on the scientific examination of biological specimens. Students learn how laboratory tests can provide important information that helps healthcare professionals detect, diagnose and monitor diseases.

The programme combines scientific knowledge with practical laboratory skills and careful analysis. Students may work with areas such as blood science, microbiology, clinical chemistry, immunology and pathology.

Main Areas of Study

Haematology Microbiology Clinical Chemistry Immunology Pathology Medical Laboratory Techniques Diagnostic Testing Laboratory Quality

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Course

⏱️ Typical Duration

🧪 Recommended Background

Students interested in Biology, Chemistry, laboratory science and healthcare may find this programme suitable.

🏥 Work Environment

Hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, research institutions, public-health laboratories and related scientific facilities.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. A common science background includes credits in English Language, Core Mathematics and Integrated Science, together with relevant science electives such as Chemistry, Biology, Physics and/or Elective Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
