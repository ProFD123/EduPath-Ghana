About Medicine

Medicine is the study of the human body, health, diseases and the ways illnesses can be prevented, diagnosed and treated. Medical students develop scientific knowledge and practical skills needed to care for patients.

What You May Learn

🧬 Human Biology

Learn about the structure and functions of the human body.

🦠 Diseases

Study diseases, their causes, prevention and effects on the body.

🔬 Diagnosis

Learn how medical professionals identify and investigate health conditions.

❤️ Patient Care

Develop knowledge and skills for supporting and caring for patients.
