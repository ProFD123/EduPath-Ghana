COMPUTING & TECHNOLOGY

Information Systems

Information Systems combines technology, business and data to help organisations manage information effectively.

About the Programme

Information Systems focuses on how organisations use technology, information and people to achieve their goals.

Students learn to analyse business needs, design information systems, manage databases and support digital transformation.

Main Areas of Study

Database Systems Systems Analysis Business Information Systems Programming Web Technologies Data Management IT Project Management Systems Design

Programme Information

🎓 Related SHS Courses

General Science, Business, Computing & ICT

⏱️ Typical Duration

Usually around 4 years for a bachelor's degree.

💻 Recommended Skills

Logical thinking, technology interest, problem-solving and communication.

🏢 Work Environment

Technology companies, banks, government agencies, businesses, consulting firms and other organisations.

Typical WASSCE Subject Requirements

Requirements vary between universities. For example, University of Ghana's Doctor of Pharmacy requires credit passes in English, Mathematics and Integrated Science, as well as Biology, Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.

Students should always check the current admission requirements of the university they want to attend.
