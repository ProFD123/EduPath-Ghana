Agricultural Science

Explore agriculture, farming, natural resources and modern approaches to food production and environmental management.

What is Agricultural Science?

Agricultural Science introduces students to the principles and practices involved in crop production, animal production, soil management and agricultural resources. It combines scientific knowledge with practical applications in agriculture.

Common Subjects

English Language Core Mathematics Integrated Science Social Studies Agricultural Science Biology Chemistry Physics Elective Mathematics

Who is Agricultural Science suitable for?

🌱 Agriculture Lovers

Students interested in farming, crops, animals and food production.

🔬 Science Enthusiasts

Students interested in applying science to agriculture and natural resources.

🌍 Environment Lovers

Students interested in natural resources, environmental management and sustainability.

🚜 Future Agripreneurs

Students interested in creating businesses and opportunities within agriculture.
